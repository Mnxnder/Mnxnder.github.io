iPhone, iPad and Android

Put it on your home screen

It then opens full screen like a normal app, with no address bar, and keeps working with no signal.

  1. iPhone / iPad: open this page in Safari, tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
  2. Android: open it in Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu, then Install app or Add to Home screen.
  3. Open it once while you have signal. After that it loads with no connection at all.

This is the only way to study on an iPhone. Safari refuses to run a downloaded HTML file, so the Windows installer and the plain app file below are for laptops.

Keep your progress

Moving your locked-in facts

Progress is stored per browser, so this website, a home-screen app and an installed laptop copy each keep their own. Sync them whenever you like.

  1. On the copy you've been studying, open Method → Studying on another laptop → Save progress file.
  2. Move that mastery-progress-….json file across by AirDrop, email, USB or OneDrive.
  3. On the other copy, open the same section and choose Load progress file.

Loading merges rather than overwrites. For every fact it keeps whichever copy got further, so studying on your phone and your laptop both count.
